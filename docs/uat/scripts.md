# Pilot user task scripts

Step 12.8. Real tasks for each kind of pilot user on staging, in plain words. Give each person only their own
section. Don't explain the screens; watch where they hesitate. Each task has a "done when" so you know they finished.

Before a session: create their account on staging (or let them join), make sure they can read the staging emails
and text messages (the staging mail catcher), and tell them nothing they do is real: no money moves and no supplier
is really contacted.

After a session: write what happened in the feedback tracker (`docs/uat/results.md`): the task, what went wrong,
the screen, how bad (blocks the task, slows it, cosmetic).

## Visitor (no account)

1. Find out which Indian states have green hydrogen policies, and which offers the most support. _Done when they name
   a state and where they read it._
2. Find a tender for electrolysers that is still open and note its closing date. _Done when they read the date._
3. Find three suppliers of hydrogen compressors in Gujarat or Maharashtra. _Done when they have three names._
4. Sign up for the weekly newsletter. _Done when they have clicked the link in the email._

## Member

1. Join H2 India as an individual. _Done when they reach their member home._
2. Set up an alert for new tenders about fuel cells, sent by email once a week. _Done when the alert shows as on._
3. Make your profile visible to other members, then hide it again. _Done when the profile says Private._
4. Download a copy of everything H2 India holds about you. _Done when they open the file._

## Buyer requester

1. Ask three suppliers for a quote for a 1 MW PEM electrolyser delivered to your plant. _Done when the request is sent._
2. Compare the quotes you receive and pick the best one; ask the supplier one question first. _Done when the question
   is answered and they have chosen._
3. (Orders on) Order a fixed-price item from its product page and pay online. _Done when the order shows Payment
   received._

## Buyer approver

1. An order from your colleague is waiting for you. Check it and approve it. _Done when it shows Approved._
2. Refuse the next one and say why. _Done when the requester can read the reason._

## Supplier admin

1. Join H2 India for your company and get verified. _Done when the checklist shows the verification request sent._
2. Put one product live, with its specifications and a datasheet. _Done when the listing is live._
3. Invite a colleague as Sales. _Done when they have joined._
4. Answer a buyer's request for a quote with a price, delivery time and payment terms. _Done when the quote is sent._
5. (Orders on) Accept an order, prepare the dispatch paperwork and mark it shipped. _Done when it shows In transit._
6. Change the bank account your payouts go to. _Done when it shows the hold and the date it ends._

## Supplier user (Sales)

1. Find the requests for quotes waiting for your company and answer one. _Done when the quote is sent._
2. Reply to a buyer's message. _Done when the buyer sees the reply._

## Receiving contact (on a phone, from the text message)

1. A delivery has arrived. Record what arrived, with a photo of any damage. _Done when the receipt shows as recorded._

## Operator

1. Check the waiting supplier verifications and approve one with its evidence. _Done when the supplier is Verified._
2. Send back a listing that is missing its datasheet, with the reason. _Done when the supplier sees the reason._
3. A buyer and a supplier disagree about a damaged delivery. Read both sides and decide. _Done when the dispute shows
   the outcome._
4. Publish a news article written by the content editor. _Done when it is on the news page._
