# Help centre: draft answers

Step 13.5. Drafts of the twenty questions people are most likely to ask, taken from the flows. **Not live**: an
editor reviews each, fixes the wording, and enters it as a Help article in the CMS (`help-articles`, with its
category); the founder approves the set (M20). Nothing here states a price, fee, time limit or legal promise that
isn't already decided; where one is pending, the draft says so in brackets.

## Signing in and accounts

**1. How do I sign in?**
Enter your work email and we'll send you a six-digit code. If you've set up a passkey on this device, use it instead:
it's faster and safer. You can also continue with Google if your work email is a Google account.

**2. I didn't get the sign-in code.**
Check your spam folder and that the email address is right. Codes take up to a minute. You can ask for a new one
after the wait shown on the screen. After too many wrong codes, sign-in pauses for a while; the screen says when you
can try again.

**3. I've lost access to my email or phone.**
Use "Can't sign in?" on the sign-in page. You can prove who you are with your passkey, your mobile number, or, if
neither works, a support request with your work ID. Support checks it with your company before changing anything.

**4. What is a passkey?**
A passkey lets you sign in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN instead of a code. It never leaves your device,
so nobody can phish it. Set one up after signing in, when we offer it.

**5. How do I add a colleague to my company?**
Company admins invite people from the Team page with their work email and a role. The invitation works only for that
email address.

## Buying

**6. How do I ask suppliers for a price?**
Use "Request a quote" on a product or supplier, or start an RFQ from the marketplace. Describe what you need, choose
up to the number of suppliers shown, and send. Our team checks each RFQ before suppliers see it.

**7. Why don't I see prices on most products?**
Most industrial equipment is priced per project, so suppliers quote for your requirement. Products with a fixed price
show it on the product page.

**8. How do I compare quotes?**
Open the RFQ: the comparison shows each supplier's latest quote side by side, with totals including GST. You can ask
a supplier a question before you decide.

**9. Who approves an order in my company?**
Your company admin sets the approval rules (for example, orders above an amount go to an approver). The approver
gets an email and approves in H2 India, with a passkey if your company asks for one.

**10. How do I pay for an order?** _(when ordering opens, Launch 2)_
After the supplier accepts, pay online or by bank transfer to the order's own account shown on the order. Never pay
into an account sent by email or message: H2 India's payment details always come from the order page.

**11. The delivery arrived damaged. What do I do?**
Record what arrived with photos using the delivery link sent to your receiving contact, then report a problem on the
order. The supplier can reply or make an offer; if you can't agree, our team decides. Refunds go back to the way you
paid.

**12. The receiving contact didn't get the delivery code.**
Ask the supplier or our team to resend it from the order. We'll never ask you to read a code out over the phone.

## Selling

**13. How do I get my company verified?**
Complete the checklist in your supplier workspace: company details, GSTIN, documents and a payout account. Our team
checks them and grants the Verified supplier badge. You'll get an email either way.

**14. How do I list a product?**
Add it from Products: choose the category, fill in its specifications and attach a datasheet. Listings are reviewed
before they go live; you'll see any changes we ask for.

**15. How do I answer an RFQ?**
New RFQs sent to your company appear in RFQs. Open one, fill in prices, delivery and payment terms, and send. You can
revise a quote; the buyer always sees the latest version you sent.

**16. When do I get paid?** _(Launch 2)_
After the buyer accepts the delivery (or, on a split schedule, when the buyer pays an advance), the amount less fees
and taxes is paid in the next payout run. [Fees and the run times on Pricing once confirmed: C1, C3, M16.]

**17. Why is my new bank account on hold?**
For your safety, a new payout account waits for a hold period before payouts use it, and we tell your admins. If you
didn't make the change, contact us at once.

## Members, news and data

**18. How do I set up an alert for tenders?**
Open Tenders, filter what you're interested in, and choose Create alert. Pick how often you want it, by email or
WhatsApp. You can pause or turn off any alert from its email.

**19. How do I unsubscribe from the newsletter?**
Use the unsubscribe link at the end of any issue; it takes one click. To stop everything except sign-in and security
emails, use "Unsubscribe from everything" in your preferences.

**20. How do I get a copy of my data, or delete my account?**
Members can download their data from their account. To correct or delete your data, or if you have a complaint,
write to the grievance officer named on the Grievance officer page; we acknowledge and answer within the times shown
there.
