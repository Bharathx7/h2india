# Grievance deadline at risk

**Trigger.** Admin › Compliance › Grievances shows a ticket amber or red (the acknowledge and resolve clocks); the
escalation email; a person writing again about the same ticket.

**Impact.** The IT Rules and DPDP require acknowledging a grievance and resolving it in time; a missed deadline is
a compliance failure and a likely complaint to the Board.

## First 15 minutes

1. Admin › Compliance › Grievances, filter by amber and red; open the oldest.
2. If it isn't acknowledged yet, acknowledge it now (the person is emailed with the resolve-by date).

## Diagnosis

- Who owns it (team) and what is it waiting for: the supplier, a document, a decision?
- Is it a data-rights request in disguise (access, correction, erasure)? Then also open it in Data rights.

## Fix

- Move it to the right team, or decide it, through the grievance's own actions (status changes are audited).
- If it truly can't be resolved in time, write to the person before the deadline: what's happening and when.
- The grievance officer is named in Company and legal details (M2); they sign off every resolution.

Rollback: none; every step is recorded.

## Confirm

The ticket shows Resolved with the outcome sent; the clocks stop.

## Who to tell

The grievance officer; the founder for anything that reached red.

## Follow-up

Count missed or near-missed deadlines monthly; if they recur, name a backup owner per team.
