## Page speed

`tests/e2e/perf/vitals.spec.ts` measures home, a news article, a category listing, a product page, a scheme and the
tender listing on a Pixel 7 profile with the network throttled to 4G (9 Mbit/s down, 1.5 up, 150 ms) and the CPU
slowed four times. It fails softly on each target and writes `test-results/perf/vitals.json`.

## Load

with filters), `product.js`, `rfq.js` (the wizard and its lookups; sending is covered by flow A) and `sign-in.js`
(the page and asking for a code; 429 answers from the per-IP limit count as correct). Each ramps to `VUS` visitors
